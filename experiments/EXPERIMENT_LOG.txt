================================================================================
GOALS & SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

PRIMARY TARGETS:
  - Inter-patient accuracy:  >= 93% (AAMI DS1 train / DS2 test)
  - Intra-patient accuracy:  >= 96% (stratified 80/20 split of all records)
  - Total system energy:     < 50 nJ per beat (frontend + classifier)
  - Total system energy:     < 30 nJ preferred if accuracy allows

HARDWARE CONSTRAINTS:
  - 180nm CMOS analog ASIC (memristive crossbar array)
  - 4-bit quantized weights (QAT during training)
  - Fully analog frontend — minimum circuit count
  - Must be manufacturable — no digital logic beyond basic comparators

DATASET:
  - MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database (48 records, 2-lead ECG, 360 Hz)
  - 5-class AAMI: N (Normal), S (Supraventricular), V (Ventricular),
                   F (Fusion), Q (Unknown/Paced)
  - Inter-patient: DS1 (22 records) train, DS2 (21 records) test
  - Class imbalance: N=45854, S=944, V=3788, F=414, Q=8 in DS1

REFERENCE BENCHMARKS:
  - Random Forest (16 features): 93.61% inter-patient (feature ceiling)
  - Published SOTA for inter-patient: 85-95% range
  - RF S-class F1: 0.05-0.09 (fundamentally hard even for RF)

PROVEN BEST CONFIGURATION (as of 2026-09-12)
================================================================================

  Result:        91.45% inter-patient accuracy
  Energy:        42.7 nJ total system (29.0 nJ classifier + 13.7 nJ frontend)
  Script:        experiments/exp_b_lean_features.py
  Confirmed by:  experiments/architecture_compare.py (Arch 1)

  ARCHITECTURE:
    16 -> BatchNorm -> 48 RLeaky -> 24 RLeaky -> 5 Leaky
    Params: 5,107
    Timesteps: 20
    Beta: 0.9 (learnable)
    Dropout: 0.1

  FEATURES (16 total, ~11 analog circuits):
    Timing (6):      pre_rr, post_rr, rr_ratio, rr_asymmetry,
                     compensatory_ratio, rr_std_10
    Morphology (4):  qrs_width_L0, qrs_width_L1, qrs_area_L0, qrs_area_L1
    Slope (2):       max_slope_L0, max_slope_L1
    Adaptive (2):    rel_area_L0, rel_area_L1
    Template (2):    templ_corr_L0, templ_corr_L1

  TRAINING PIPELINE:
    - Training-CE checkpoint (NOT val-based — DS1 val does NOT predict DS2)
    - Spike-count prediction: spk_out.sum(dim=0).max(1) (NOT mem_out[-1].argmax)
    - SMOTE 0.33 oversampling on all 5 classes
    - Focal loss gamma=2.0
    - Class weighting: cls_power=0.65
    - 4-bit QAT with STE (quantize during forward, restore for gradient)
    - Adam LR=1e-3, weight_decay=1e-4
    - Cosine annealing with 5-epoch warmup
    - Sparsity penalty: lambda=1.0, target fire_rate=0.15
    - 200 epochs, batch_size=128
    - Grad clip 1.0

  FEATURE EXTRACTION:
    - EWMA gated update (only when templ_corr > 0.85)
    - EWMA alpha = 0.015 for morphology
    - EWMA init from median of first 10 beats
    - 10-beat local RR window
    - QRS window: samples 70-130 (60 samples at 360 Hz)
    - Beat window: 90 left, 108 right of R-peak
    - Template: 6-sample downsampled QRS for correlation

  CONFUSION MATRIX (inter-patient):

    - 885 V beats misclassified as N (27.5% of V beats lost)

    1. Wrong prediction method: mem_out[-1].argmax(1) instead of
       spk_out.sum(dim=0).max(1) — single noisy snapshot vs temporal vote
    2. Wrong checkpoint: val-based (DS1 holdout) instead of training-CE
    3. Wrong features: 14 features (shared_data.py) instead of proven 16
       - shared_data.py dropped: compensatory_ratio, qrs_width_L0,
         max_slope_L1, qrs_width_L1, qrs_area_L1
       - shared_data.py added: slope_ratio_L0, rel_peak_L0, corr_L0L1

  ARCH 1: SNN-Optimized (48->24, 14 features)
    Script: experiments/arch_1_snn_optimized.py
    Result: 74.73% — catastrophic regression from 91.45%
    Cause: All 3 methodology errors above

  ARCH 2: Pure ANN (48->24 ReLU, 14 features)
    Script: experiments/arch_2_ann_direct.py
    Result: 85.43%
    Note: ANN was higher than SNN only due to methodology errors in SNN

  ARCH 3: Hybrid ANN->SNN (14->32 ReLU -> 24 RLeaky -> 5)
    Script: experiments/arch_3_hybrid.py
    Result: 79.60%

  ARCH 4: SNN-Temporal (5-beat sequence, one per timestep)
    Script: experiments/arch_4_snn_temporal.py
    Result: 57.94%
    Cause: SMOTE on 5-beat sequences from 8 Q-class samples = pure noise

  ARCH 5: SNN-Wide (64->32, 14 features)
    Script: experiments/arch_5_snn_wide.py
    Result: 78.96%

  ARCH 6: Hybrid-Temporal (ANN encoder + temporal SNN)
    Script: experiments/arch_6_hybrid_temporal.py
    Result: 56.50%

  ARCH 7: Dual-Pathway (timing fast + morphology slow)
    Script: experiments/arch_7_dual_pathway.py
    Result: 66.64%

  ARCH 8: Skip-Residual (raw features bypass to all layers, 32->16)
    Script: experiments/arch_8_skip_residual.py
    Result: 79.09%

  ARCH 9: Multi-Timescale Temporal (dual timescale + 5-beat sequence)
    Script: experiments/arch_9_multiscale_temporal.py

  LESSON: These results are ALL INVALID for architecture comparison because
  they share 3 methodology errors. The only valid conclusion: temporal
  sequence processing (feeding 5 beats one per timestep) is catastrophically

PHASE 2: CONSOLIDATED ARCHITECTURE COMPARISON (on proven foundation)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Script: experiments/architecture_compare.py
  Method: Exact Exp B training pipeline (training-CE checkpoint, spike-count
          prediction, 16 features, SMOTE 0.33, etc.) applied to 6 architectures

  ARCH 1: SNN Baseline (16->48->24->5)
    Result: 91.45% inter | 42.7 nJ total | Fire: 0.165
    Status: CONFIRMED baseline

  ARCH 2: Pure ANN (16->48 ReLU->24 ReLU->5)
    Result: 76.20% inter | 17.8 nJ total | Fire: N/A
    Finding: SNN temporal dynamics add +15.25% over ANN
    Lesson: SPIKE-COUNT TEMPORAL INTEGRATION IS ESSENTIAL

  ARCH 3: Hybrid ANN32->SNN24 (16->32 ReLU->24 RLeaky->5)
    Result: 90.88% inter | 43.4 nJ total | Fire: 0.288
    Finding: Pure SNN beats hybrid by +0.57%
    Lesson: ANN encoder doesn't help — raw features into SNN is better

  ARCH 4: Skip-SNN (16->32 RLeaky, [16+32]->16 RLeaky, [16+16]->5)
    Result: 89.95% inter | 28.3 nJ total | Fire: 0.181
    Finding: F recall 47% (vs 12% baseline!), S recall 9% (vs 2%)
    Finding: V recall 72% (vs 63%), N recall 95% (vs 98%)
    Lesson: SKIP CONNECTIONS DRAMATICALLY HELP RARE CLASSES at small width
            but N recall drops due to capacity constraints (32-16 vs 48-24)

  ARCH 5: DualPathway (timing fast + morphology slow -> merge)
    Result: 79.37% inter | 36.0 nJ total | Fire: 0.204
    Lesson: FORCED FEATURE SPLITTING IS HARMFUL — network needs to jointly
            process all features

  ARCH 6: SNN Baseline + corr_L0L1 (17 features)
    Result: Was still running when session ended
    Status: INCOMPLETE

PHASE 3: GEN2 — ARCHITECTURAL + TRAINING EXPERIMENTS (all FAILED)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  All experiments regressed from 91.45% baseline.

  GEN2 EXP 1: Skip-Large (48-24 with skip connections, 16 features)
    Script: experiments/gen2_skip_large.py
    Result: 84.07% inter | 49.8 nJ total | Fire: 0.187
    Delta: -7.38% from baseline
    Confusion: N recall 90.1% (down from 98.2%), V recall 50.5% (down!)
    Root cause: At full width, skip connections create a "lazy" pathway.
      Raw features are continuous (always present), spikes are sparse (15%).
      The concatenation [raw + spikes] is dominated by raw features,
      preventing the SNN from learning useful temporal representations.
      At 32-16 (Phase 2) this helped because the network was capacity-starved.
      At 48-24 the skip is counterproductive.
    Intra: Was still running

  GEN2 EXP 2: Enhanced Training (48-24 baseline, label smoothing + mixup, 17 feat)
    Script: experiments/gen2_enhanced.py
    Result: 84.98% inter | 51.0 nJ total | Fire: 0.215
    Delta: -6.47% from baseline
    Root cause: CE NEVER DROPPED BELOW 5.0 (vs 0.6-0.8 for baseline).
      Mixup is FUNDAMENTALLY INCOMPATIBLE with SNN temporal dynamics.
      Mixed inputs (interpolated between two heartbeats) don't produce
      meaningful spike patterns — the temporal integration has nothing
      coherent to integrate. Label smoothing may have also hurt.
    Intra: Was still running

  GEN2 EXP 3: Combined (Skip-48-24 + label smoothing + mixup, 17 features)
    Script: experiments/gen2_combined.py
    Result: 83.74% inter | 52.5 nJ total | Fire: 0.200
    Delta: -7.71% from baseline
    Root cause: Both problems from Exp 1 and Exp 2 compound.
    Intra: Was still running

  GEN2 EXP 4: Wide-Shallow Skip (17->64 RLeaky, [17+64]->5 Leaky, single layer)
    Script: experiments/gen2_wide_skip.py
    Result: 81.97% inter / 94.48% intra | 51.9 nJ / 48.6 nJ total
    Delta: -9.48% inter from baseline
    Finding: Single hidden layer is insufficient for inter-patient
    Finding: 94.48% intra is decent — architecture works when patient-specific
             patterns are available in training set
    Finding: Best minority class detection among Gen2:
             S recall 28%, V recall 69%, F recall 70%
    Lesson: Wide+skip helps rare classes (F=70%!) but devastates N (85%)

  SUMMARY TABLE (Phase 3):

================================================================================
KEY PROVEN FACTS (do NOT re-test)
================================================================================

  1. SNN with spike-count temporal integration is essential (+15% over ANN)
  2. Training-CE checkpoint beats val-based checkpoint for inter-patient
  3. spk_out.sum(dim=0).max(1) beats mem_out[-1].argmax(1)
  4. 16-feature set (Exp B) is the proven best feature set
  5. SMOTE 0.33 is better than SMOTE 0.5 (prior experiment)
  6. Feature changes must be ADDITIVE to proven 16 (not swap)
  7. Mixup augmentation is INCOMPATIBLE with SNN (CE stuck at 5x normal)
  8. Skip connections at full 48-24 width are HARMFUL (lazy pathway)
  9. Skip connections at 32-16 width HELP rare classes (F recall 47%)
  10. Forced feature splitting (DualPathway) is HARMFUL
  11. Hybrid ANN->SNN is worse than pure SNN (-0.57%)
  12. Single hidden layer is insufficient for inter-patient
  13. Sequential beat processing (multi-beat temporal) is catastrophic
  14. S-class is fundamentally hard (F1 = 0.05-0.09 even for RF)

ENERGY BUDGET BREAKDOWN
================================================================================

  FRONTEND: 13.7 nJ per beat
    - 5 always-on circuits * 0.833 = 4.2 nJ
    - 95 computation circuits * 0.100 = 9.5 nJ

  CLASSIFIER (depends on architecture and fire rate):
    Formula: total_MACs * 2 pJ/MAC / 1000 = nJ
    For baseline 48-24 at fire_rate=0.165:
      fc1:   16 * 48 = 768 MACs (non-recurrent, once)
      rec1:  48 * 48 * 20 * 0.165 = 7,603 MACs
      mid:   48 * 24 * 20 * 0.165 = 3,802 MACs
      rec2:  24 * 24 * 20 * 0.165 = 1,901 MACs
      out:   24 * 5  * 20 * 0.165 = 396 MACs
      Total: ~14,470 MACs -> 29.0 nJ classifier

  TOTAL: 13.7 + 29.0 = 42.7 nJ (within 50 nJ budget)
