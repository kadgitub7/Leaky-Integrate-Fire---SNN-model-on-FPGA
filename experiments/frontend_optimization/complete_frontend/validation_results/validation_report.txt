14    N     8.147s    0.708V  7.393s*   -754ms*   MISATTRIBUTED

ROOT CAUSE:
Beat 9 (V-beat, V_peak=0.346V) produces TWO rising edges on peak_detected
within its ARM window (6.250-6.333s):
  - 1st RISE at 6.270s (correct, latency=17ms)
  - 2nd RISE at 6.302s (duplicate, 32ms later)

Beat 10 (V-beat, V_peak=0.300V) also produces a duplicate:
  - Actual detection at 6.616s (within ARM 6.575-6.658s, latency=38ms)
  - Duplicate at 6.654s

These 2 extra RISE events shift the .meas RISE counter, misattributing all
subsequent detections. The actual beats ARE detected — they're just counted
against the wrong .meas commands.

MECHANISM:
At very low ECG amplitude (0.300-0.346V), the source followers shift the
input to near 0V. The comparator operates at the edge of its linear range,
and the peak_detected output may oscillate or produce a secondary pulse as
the charge pump slowly tracks the small signal.

IMPACT:
The peak detector DOES detect all beats, including the low-amplitude V-beats.
The issue is duplicate output pulses, not missed detections. In the full
frontend, the downstream edge_buffer + pulse_gen chain would need blanking
to prevent double triggers.

RECOMMENDED FIX (for after validation stage):
Add output blanking: once peak_detected goes HIGH, hold for a minimum
refractory period (e.g., 50ms) before allowing another rising edge.
This can be a simple RC + comparator or an SR latch with delayed reset.

DECISION: CONDITIONAL PASS

Justification:
- The v6 true peak detector successfully detects ALL beats across 5 diverse
  records, including V-beats, high noise, irregular rhythm, and sharp QRS.
- 60/75 beats (80%) have clean, unambiguous detection with consistent latency.
- 9/75 beats (12%) are Record 203 N-beats with correct detection but higher latency.
- 6/75 beats (8%) are affected by V-beat duplicate triggers in Record 203.
- The duplicate trigger issue is an OUTPUT BLANKING problem, not a detection
  failure. All beats are detected; the extra pulses can be suppressed downstream.

Conditions for proceeding:
1. Add output blanking (refractory period) to true_peak_detector before
   integration with the full frontend.
2. OR: rely on the downstream edge_buffer/pulse_gen chain to debounce,
   and verify that the existing chain already rejects pulses < 50ms apart.
